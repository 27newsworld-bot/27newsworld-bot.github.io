About the app

What 27 News HQ is

27 News HQ is a private production tool of the news channel 27 News World (YouTube and Instagram @27news_world, TikTok @27.news.world). It runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and no other person or account can sign in to it.

The tool connects only to the channel's own accounts. It reads the channel's statistics and comments, and it sends the channel's own news videos to its accounts after the owner has approved each video.

Terms of Service

Who may use it

Only the owner of 27 News World uses 27 News HQ, and only for the channel's own accounts. There are no other users, no sign-up and no paid service.

Platform rules

Every post made with the tool follows the terms and community guidelines of the platform it goes to, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, YouTube's Terms of Service and Instagram's Terms of Use. Videos made with an AI voice or AI images are labelled as such on every platform.

Content

The channel owner is responsible for every video and caption. Each video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is sent to a platform. Sources are named on screen and in the description.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is for the channel's internal use, without any warranty.

Changes

These terms may change when the tool changes. The date at the top shows the current version.

Privacy Policy

Data the tool handles

  • TikTok (only the channel account, after the owner authorises it): account id and display name, follower and like counts, the list of the channel's videos with their view, like, comment and share counts. The channel's own approved videos are uploaded to the account's TikTok inbox, where the owner finishes the post in the TikTok app.
  • YouTube: the channel's statistics, its videos' counts and the public comments on the channel's own videos, so the owner can answer them.
  • Instagram: the account's follower count, its posts with their counts, and publishing of the channel's own approved Reels.

How it is stored

All data stays on the channel owner's computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown, logged or sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to. The tool has no server on the internet, no advertising, no analytics and no cookies for visitors.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. Data goes only to the platform it came from, for example a video to TikTok.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting a platform in the tool deletes its stored tokens at once. Statistics are kept locally for the channel's own reporting and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access can also be withdrawn on the platform itself, for TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to 27 News World on TikTok (@27.news.world) or Instagram (@27news_world).
